Where the numbers come from

    
    
      
  • Short codes send about 100 segments per second by default; faster is a paid upgrade. Standard toll-free numbers send about 3 per second until the provider raises them. Speed counts segments, not texts. Twilio throughput guide
    • 
      
  • Providers usually set the limit for the whole account, not each number. Two blasts running at once share it, so don't add the lines together.
    • 
      
  • One segment holds 160 plain characters, or 153 each once a text runs long. A single emoji or curly quote switches the whole text to Unicode: 70 characters, then 67 each. Carriers bill per segment.
    • 
      
  • The prices on this page are examples. Replace them with the per-segment rates on your provider's usage or billing screen.
    • 
      
  • Carrier fees vary by carrier and usually have no fixed rate on a bill. Divide the total carrier fees by the number of segments they cover to get an average. They can be larger than the provider's own send fee.
    • 
      
  • Costs are before any free allowance on your plan, so the real bill can be slightly lower.
    • 
      
  • Image texts (MMS) count as one message up to 1,600 characters. Image sends may be slower than plain texts; check with your provider before a big image blast.
    • 
      
  • Times are estimates. Real sends can run a little slower when other texts are in the queue.
